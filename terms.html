Terms of use

Caregiver Kid, a prototype family app from Kian Labs. Last updated 16 November 2026.

What you're agreeing to

  • Caregiver Kid helps parents keep track of their child's day and health records. It is a record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice and is not a substitute for a doctor, pharmacist or emergency services. In an emergency, call 911.
  • You may connect MyChart only for yourself or for a child or other person you are legally allowed to access (for example, through MyChart proxy access).
  • Records brought in from MyChart are read-only copies. If something looks wrong, check with your health system; Caregiver Kid can't change your medical record.

Your data

  • Records go from MyChart to your phone to your family's own Carelog box at home. There is no Caregiver Kid cloud server.
  • Caregiver Kid reads: immunizations, vital signs (height and weight), allergies, medication orders, visits, and basic patient details to confirm whose records they are. It never writes to your health record.
  • Your MyChart password is entered only on your health system's own page. Caregiver Kid never sees it and stores no hospital login or token.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data, show ads, or track you. Only people your family adds to its box can see the records.
  • You can stop at any time: remove the connection by not syncing again, hide records in the app, or delete your box's data. To revoke access at the source, use your health system's MyChart settings.

The fine print

  • Caregiver Kid is a prototype provided as-is, without warranties. Keep your own copies of anything important.
  • These terms may change; the date above shows the latest version.
